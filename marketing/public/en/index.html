The game

A card table in your pocket

LeTer is a mobile app for multiplayer card games. Two to four players sit down at a table and play together, each on their own phone. The rules are enforced by the server, so everyone sees the same game at the same moment. Every game is played with a token stake; creating an account is free.

Fap-Fap

The game of Ter: a 31-card deck, 2 to 4 players. Each player is dealt 5 cards and the game is played over 5 tricks. One player leads the trick; the others must follow the suit led if they can. The highest card of that suit wins the trick and takes control of the next one. Think ahead, adapt your strategy and stay in control.

GARAM TIA

A 35-card deck, 2 to 4 players. Each player is dealt 3 cards, and cards are laid face up in the middle of the table: one more than the number of players (3 cards with 2 players, 4 with 3, 5 with 4). You follow the suit led if you can; the highest card of that suit takes control. Hands are then completed with the cards from the middle: the highest total without going over 21 wins.

Every turn is timed. If a player doesn't play in time, the server plays a card for them, following a fixed rule, so the game can go on.

Tokens and stakes

Playing with tokens

Every LeTer game is played with a token stake: there are no free games. The game account itself is free.

Value
1 token = 1 FCFA.
Top up
With MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money, from the app. Tokens are credited as soon as the payment is confirmed; a failed or cancelled payment credits nothing, and you can try again.
Stake
Every player at the table stakes the same amount. To join a game, your balance must be at least twice the stake: in some cases (for example a Kora win in Fap-Fap), a losing player loses twice the stake. Otherwise you cannot join and need to top up.
Win
A game has a single winner. LeTer takes a commission on the losing players’ stakes, at a rate that depends on the stake amount; the winner gets their own stake back and receives everything else. Winnings are credited to your token balance immediately.
Withdraw
From 1,000 tokens (1,000 tokens = 1,000 FCFA), to the MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money number registered on your LeTer account, and that number only. The withdrawal is processed automatically by the payment provider.

For people aged 18 and over only. Staking involves a risk of loss: only stake amounts you can afford to lose. Top-ups and withdrawals go through MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money: they are open today to Cameroonian numbers, and other countries where these services exist are planned.

How to play

Four steps to take a seat at the table

  1. Install LeTer

    Download the app from the official link published on this site.

  2. Create your account

    First name, last name, username, phone number and password; email is optional. The account is free and for people aged 18 and over. Write down your recovery code: it is shown only once.

  3. Top up your tokens

    With MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money. Keep at least twice the stake of the table you want to join.

  4. Play

    Fap-Fap or GARAM TIA: join a table, create your own or enter a tournament. The game starts with two players.

Referrals

Invite your friends and enjoy the LeTer program.

Every player has a personal referral code in the app. Share it: a friend who enters it when creating their account becomes your referral.

  1. 1

    Share your invitation

    Find your personal code under Aide et réclamations (Help and complaints), in the Parrainage (Referral) tab. Copy it or share your invitation link.

  2. 2

    Your friend joins LeTer

    They create a new account and enter your code in the “Code de parrainage” (referral code) field. An existing account cannot become a referral.

  3. 3

    Qualification is checked

    The server checks each referral's qualifying conditions, works out the tiers reached and credits the rewards, under the program rules.

Program rules

Qualified referral
Their own confirmed token purchases reach 1,000 tokens in total. Purchases made by different referrals don't add up.
One tier
10 qualified referrals.
Reward per tier
5,000 tokens per completed tier: 10 qualified referrals → 5,000 tokens, 20 → 10,000 in total, 30 → 15,000 in total.
Conditions
One referrer per referral; no self-referral; only referrals whose account is active and in good standing are counted. The program may be suspended, or its rules adjusted for future tiers; a tier already reached is kept.

Reward crediting is not active yet. Tiers reached are recorded and will be credited once the program is finally approved. No reward is due unless the conditions above are met.

Rewards are credited as tokens to your LeTer game account. The rules in force are those shown in the app.

Tournaments and competition

Measure yourself against other players

Tournaments have a start and an end date. Sign up before the tournament starts, then join a table of 4 players during the playing period.

At closing, once every game has ended, the server calculates the official ranking and publishes it in the app; it does not change after that.

You can also propose a tournament from the app: it is published once the LeTer team has approved it.

The schedule and conditions of each tournament (eligibility, format, rewards) are shown in the app before you sign up.

Support

Frequently asked questions

What is LeTer?

A mobile app for multiplayer card games: Fap-Fap and GARAM TIA, two to four players per table, in real time, with token stakes, tournaments and a referral program.

How do I create an account?

In the app, choose “Inscription” (Sign up): first name, last name, username, phone number (Cameroon +237 preselected, can be changed) and password. Email is optional. The account is free and for people aged 18 and over. Keep the recovery code shown at the end in a safe place: you will need it if you forget your password.

How much does a game cost?

The stake is set when the table is created and every player stakes the same amount, in tokens (1 token = 1 FCFA). Your balance must be at least twice the stake to join the game. See the Tokens and stakes section.

How do I withdraw my winnings?

From the app, from 1,000 tokens: the amount is sent to the MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money number registered on your LeTer account. Withdrawing to another number is not possible.

How does multiplayer work?

Each table seats 2, 3 or 4 players, set when it is created. Seats are fixed and play goes counter-clockwise. The server applies the rules and sends the game to every player in real time.

How does the referral program work?

Your personal code is under Aide et réclamations (Help and complaints), in the Parrainage (Referral) tab. Your friend enters it when creating their account. If you received an invitation link, write down the code it shows: after installing the app, enter it when you sign up. The detailed rules are in the Referrals section.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose “Mot de passe oublié ?” (Forgot password?), then enter your phone number or email and your recovery code. If you have lost this code, contact support.

Where can I get help if something goes wrong?

In the app: Profile, then Aide et réclamations (Help and complaints). You can file a complaint there and follow the reply.

To email support, use the address given in the legal notice.
